Instructions

up-arrow: move up, down-arrow: move down

left-arrow: move left, right-arrow: move right

when Rotate/Scale Mode is [Checked]

up-arrow: scale up, down-arrow: scale down

left-arrow: rotate counter-clockwise, right-arrow: rotate clockwise

Press R key for Red color, B key for Blue, G key for Green

Press Y key for Yellow color, O key for Orange, P key for Purple
